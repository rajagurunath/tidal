\caption[The A-ladder]{%
  \sloppy\textbf{The A-ladder, drawn.} The four rungs of \cref{tab:taxonomy}, in
  order of widening scope: A0 places deferrable work inside one aggregated engine,
  A-F across the replicas of a fleet, A-D into the idle \emph{phase pool} of a
  disaggregated deployment, and A-P across \emph{time} itself, by freezing a
  finished prefill's KV to a durable store and thawing it into a decode worker
  hours later. Each panel carries the advantage that rung buys and the number that
  evidences it; A-D is drawn dimmed because it is design plus a metric mapping
  (\cref{tab:dynamo}) rather than a measurement. The last rung makes the section's
  one-liner literal: \emph{disaggregation separates prefill from decode in space;
  a deadline-contracted batch tier separates them in time.}}
\label{fig:aladder}
